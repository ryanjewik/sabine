As of the 2025 competitive season, the official VALORANT map pool consists of the following 7 maps:

1. Ascent
2. Bind
3. Haven
4. Icebox
5. Lotus
6. Sunset
7. Abyss

These maps are featured in VCT and ranked Competitive play. Riot rotates the map pool periodically to ensure strategic diversity.

---

COMMON AGENT COMPOSITIONS BY MAP:

**Ascent**
- Typical comp: Omen / Sova / Killjoy / Jett / KAY/O
- Notes: Ascent emphasizes mid control and reliable retake utility. Killjoy holds sites well, and Sova/KAY/O provide info and suppression.

**Bind**
- Typical comp: Raze / Skye / Viper / Brimstone / Cypher
- Notes: Tight corners favor Raze. Skye enables fast site hits. Double controller (Viper + Brim) helps control choke-heavy areas. Cypher anchors and flanks.

**Haven**
- Typical comp: Jett / Breach / Omen / Killjoy / Sova
- Notes: Haven has three sites, so flexible agents are key. Breach assists in site retakes. Killjoy anchors one site. Jett provides early picks.

**Icebox**
- Typical comp: Viper / Sova / Killjoy / Sage / Jett
- Notes: Post-plant utility is critical on Icebox. Viper walls enable spike plants. Sage slows pushes. Sova and Killjoy control space and gather info.

**Lotus**
- Typical comp: Raze / Fade / Omen / Killjoy / Skye
- Notes: Lotus’s rotating doors and three sites benefit fast, flexible agents. Fade reveals enemy positions. Raze clears space. Killjoy anchors.

**Sunset**
- Typical comp: Raze / KAY/O / Omen / Cypher / Skye
- Notes: A traditional control map. Raze and Skye help with utility clearing. KAY/O suppresses defenders. Cypher watches flanks.

**Abyss**
- Typical comp: Jett / Omen / Sova / Sage / Chamber
- Notes: Unique fall hazards encourage long-range picks and vertical play. Chamber or Jett often anchor long angles. Sova and Sage support site hits.

---

Compositions vary slightly by region and meta, but core agent roles — one duelist, one initiator, one sentinel, and one or two controllers — are common. Teams adjust comps based on comfort, strategy, and counter-picks.
