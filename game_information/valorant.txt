VALORANT is a free-to-play 5v5 tactical first-person shooter (FPS) developed and published by Riot Games. It was officially released on June 2, 2020, for Windows PC.

In VALORANT, two teams of five players compete in objective-based rounds. One team plays as the attackers and the other as the defenders. The main objective is to plant or defuse a device called the "Spike" or to eliminate the opposing team.

Each match consists of up to 25 rounds, and the first team to win 13 rounds wins the match. Teams switch roles (attacker/defender) after 12 rounds.

Players choose from a roster of characters known as "agents." Each agent has a unique set of abilities, including one ultimate ability that charges over time or by completing specific in-game actions.

There are different roles for agents: Duelists (entry fraggers), Initiators (disrupt defenders), Sentinels (lock down areas), and Controllers (manipulate space). Strategic team composition and coordination are critical.

The game emphasizes precise aim, team communication, economy management (buying weapons and abilities), and map control. Maps have distinct layouts with choke points, spike sites, and elevation differences.

VALORANT is played professionally in regional leagues and international tournaments under the VALORANT Champions Tour (VCT) organized by Riot Games.

Popular game modes include Unrated, Competitive (ranked), Spike Rush (short mode), Deathmatch (warm-up), and Custom games for practice or scrims.
