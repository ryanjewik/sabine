In VALORANT, agents are divided into four primary roles: Duelists, Initiators, Sentinels, and Controllers. Each role reflects the agent’s intended playstyle and utility in team compositions.

---

**Duelists**  
Role: Aggressive fraggers who create space and take opening duels.  
These agents are self-sufficient and excel at getting early kills.

- Jett
- Reyna
- Phoenix
- Raze
- Yoru
- Neon
- Iso
- Waylay

---

**Initiators**  
Role: Disrupt defenders and gather information to help the team enter sites.  
They use abilities to force movement, scout positions, and support executes.

- Sova
- Breach
- Skye
- Fade
- KAY/O
- Gekko
- Tejo

---

**Sentinels**  
Role: Defensive experts who lock down areas and protect flanks.  
They provide healing, traps, or turret utility to anchor sites and hold space.

- Sage
- Killjoy
- Cypher
- Chamber
- Deadlock
- Vyse

---

**Controllers**  
Role: Map control specialists who shape the battlefield.  
They use smokes, walls, and other utility to block vision and delay pushes.

- Brimstone
- Omen
- Viper
- Astra
- Harbor
- Clove

---

Each role is critical to balanced team composition. Most professional and ranked teams run one or two Controllers, one Sentinel, one Initiator, and one Duelist, depending on map and strategy.
