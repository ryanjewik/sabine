The VALORANT Champions Tour (VCT) is the official professional esports circuit for VALORANT, run by Riot Games. It features regional leagues, global tournaments, and an end-of-year world championship.

As of 2024, the VCT includes four major regions:
- Americas
- EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa)
- Pacific
- China

Each region runs its own **International League**, where top teams compete in split seasons (Stage 1, Stage 2). At the end of each stage, the best teams qualify for international tournaments.

**Key VCT Tiers and Events:**

1. **International Leagues**  
   Franchised teams compete in their regional leagues. The top performers advance to global events.

2. **Masters**  
   Mid-year international tournaments where top teams from each region face off. Performance at Masters impacts qualification for Champions.

3. **Champions**  
   The end-of-year global finals. It is the most prestigious VALORANT event, where the top teams compete for the world title.

4. **Challengers & Ascension**  
   Tier-2 ecosystem for aspiring teams. Regional Challengers leagues lead to the Ascension tournament. Winners of Ascension earn a temporary slot in the next year’s International League.

China now has a dedicated slot in the international circuit. Its teams participate directly in global events such as Masters and Champions, with a separate qualification pathway.

The VCT ecosystem supports year-round competition, with structured progression from amateur to professional levels, culminating in VALORANT Champions.
